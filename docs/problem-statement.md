# Problem Statement

## Role Preference
Forward-Deployed Engineering

## Business
Papahandmadeit is an African fashion and visual-art brand that creates handmade clothing and limited collections inspired by African culture, identity and aesthetics.

## Proposed Agent and Why It Matters
I want to build a Whatsapp agent that checks product availability, helps the customer reserve an available item, generates and verifies a test payment, and records a simple fulfillment preference. This is worth solving because faster responses and reliable reservations can reduce missed sales while reducing the owner's manual workload during high-demand drops.

## Deliberate Scope Limit
I am deliberately leaving out a full website or e-commerce platform, delivery tracking, and other production features beyond the limited-drop reservation and payment workflow.
